## Purpose

Provide a durable, reusable classification layer for David Potgieter's virtual-meeting corpus so future sales-methodology research can distinguish actual sales/business-development conversations from candidate interviews, service delivery, internal planning, vendor procurement and other non-sales meetings.

This is **not** a CRM and does not store transcript content. SharePoint/OneDrive remains the source for the private transcript. This register stores only the minimum classification metadata needed for research and retrieval.

## Privacy boundary

`tbhrc/drf-main` is public. Do not copy transcript text, personal contact details, private SharePoint URLs, salary details, client-confidential information or other unnecessary personal data into this file.

Each row uses a SharePoint **item ID** or a stable source title/search key so an authorised agent can retrieve the original transcript through the Microsoft SharePoint connector when needed.

## Classification contract

### Primary classification

- **Sales / Business Development** — David/Talent Bridge/iMPLEMENTAi/B-Change is actively diagnosing, positioning, offering, pricing, pitching, qualifying, negotiating, expanding or structuring a commercial service/partnership.
- **Non-sales** — the meeting is primarily delivery, candidate interviewing, internal/partner operating work, vendor procurement, peer networking or another activity where David is not selling a service in that meeting.

### Non-sales subtypes

- Candidate interview / recruitment delivery
- Existing-client coaching / service delivery
- Internal / partner commercial planning
- Vendor / procurement discovery
- Venture / operating strategy
- Peer networking / relationship

### Sales stage/context

Use the smallest accurate stage evidenced by the transcript:

- First discovery / qualification
- Diagnostic / needs analysis
- Service presentation / education
- Pricing / proposal
- Negotiation / agreement
- Partnership / channel development
- Existing relationship expansion

### Service taxonomy

Use one or more of these service families when sales-related:

1. **Recruitment / Executive Search / Headhunting**
2. **Bulk Recruitment / Staffing**
3. **Recruitment Partnership / RPO / Outsourced Delivery**
4. **Psychometric / Candidate Assessment / Job-Fit Assessment**
5. **Career Consulting / Executive Coaching / Profile Transformation**
6. **iMPLEMENTAi AI Strategy / Automation / AI Implementation**
7. **Organisation / Leadership / Business Consulting**
8. **Corporate Training / Workshops**
9. **Other clearly evidenced commercial proposition**

## What the corpus currently shows

### Sales motions found

The reviewed sales meetings are not one homogeneous category. They currently cluster into five repeatable motions:

1. **Direct employer recruitment sales** — employer has hiring need; David diagnoses role/volume, teaches process, qualifies budget/terms and moves to proposal/agreement.
2. **Recruitment/RPO partnerships** — another recruitment or delivery partner wants UAE capability, fulfilment capacity or a revenue-share arrangement.
3. **Assessment sales** — psychometric/job-fit assessment requirements are scoped as a separate paid service or recruitment adjunct.
4. **Career-package sales** — free consultation/value-first review leads into executive coaching, profile transformation, job-search strategy, market research or a bounded first paid step.
5. **iMPLEMENTAi / consulting sales** — diagnosis of operational/commercial pain leads to AI strategy, automation, implementation, workshop or broader organisational-consulting options.

### Critical negative controls

The following must **not** be classified as sales merely because they contain sales, recruitment, pricing or business language:

- candidate interviews for an existing client vacancy;
- paid coaching/service-delivery sessions;
- internal partner fee/revenue-share planning;
- vendor calls where David is the buyer;
- venture planning / operating design;
- peer networking without a clear commercial offer or buying step.

This distinction is important for future methodology extraction: only meetings classified **Sales / Business Development** should enter the primary sales-behaviour corpus by default.

## Reusable classification rule

For every newly discovered transcript:

1. **Read the transcript. Do not classify from the filename alone.**
2. Identify who is buying, who is selling and the meeting's primary purpose.
3. Assign `Sales / Business Development` or `Non-sales`.
4. If sales-related, assign the exact service family/families actually offered.
5. Assign the smallest accurate commercial stage/context.
6. Record one concise evidence-safe reason.
7. Record the SharePoint item ID or stable search key, not transcript text/private URL.
8. Search this register for the item ID/title before adding a row so duplicate SharePoint copies do not create duplicate meetings.

## Discovery/search contract for future refreshes

Use bounded SharePoint searches such as:

- `David transcript`
- `David meeting transcript`
- `"David x" transcript`
- `x David transcript`
- `Discovery transcript`
- `Consultation transcript`
- `"Free Recruitment Consultation" David`

Then deduplicate by meeting identity/item content before classification.

Do not claim the corpus is mathematically exhaustive unless SharePoint provides an authoritative complete meeting inventory. Search indexing can omit unindexed, renamed, deleted, non-transcribed or permission-restricted meetings. The 38-row corpus above is the **verified indexed corpus reviewed in this research pass**, not a guaranteed denominator of every meeting David has ever held.
