## Founder read

The long-standing Vertical AI OS parent now has a concrete Sniper beachhead rather than a generic integration proposition. Current global and UAE operator evidence shows that wholesale distributors pay to automate the work between email/WhatsApp/PDF orders and ERP systems. Local competitors also demonstrate setup + recurring pricing in the UAE.

The correct DRF product is not a new ERP. It is a **bounded order-desk operating layer around the existing ERP**, with deterministic validation and human exception gates for commercially material fields.

## Buyer / trigger / outcome

**Payer:** owner/GM, commercial/sales director, operations manager or distribution/supply-chain leader.

**Trigger:** staff repeatedly retype customer POs from email/WhatsApp/PDF into ERP, chase missing information, apply customer-specific SKUs/prices and manage order-status exceptions manually.

**Outcome:** reduce order-entry labour and response delay while preserving human control over ambiguous SKU, price, credit, substitution and stock decisions.

## Offer

### Distributor Order Desk AI Operating Pack

`order channel → extraction → business-rule validation → ERP draft → human exception → status response → audit / QA`

Optional quote/procurement/PO-chasing modules come later.

## Commercial model

- estimated setup/integration: **AED12,500–25,000**;
- estimated managed MRR: **AED2,500–5,000/month**;
- third-party usage/pass-through separate when material.

Local Artin WholesaleOS public pricing around AED25k–40k setup and AED1,999–3,499/month supports the general commercial band, but DRF pricing remains unaccepted until a customer commits.

## GTM

- list UAE building-material / industrial-supply distributors with visible email/WhatsApp ordering;
- order-desk diagnostic against 30–50 historic orders;
- show real customer/SKU/ERP mapping;
- require paid design-partner deposit before substantial custom integration;
- sell the measurable order-desk outcome, not “AI transformation”.

## Delivery

```text
Email / WhatsApp / PDF
→ schema extraction
→ customer/SKU/price/credit/stock checks
→ native/API ERP draft where available
→ human gate for ambiguous/material fields
→ approved response/status
→ exception + correction telemetry
```
