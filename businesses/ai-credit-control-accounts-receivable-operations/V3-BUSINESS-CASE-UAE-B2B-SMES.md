## Market-ready offer

**AI Credit Control Desk**

Promise: create one disciplined owner/cadence for normal B2B receivables, reduce avoidable overdue cash and give management a live promise/dispute/cash view without hiring a full-time credit controller.

### Forward-test commercial model

- **AED1,500 onboarding**;
- **AED1,750/month** up to roughly 200 invoices/month;
- **AED2,750/month** higher-volume tier up to roughly 500 invoices/month;
- complex disputes, legal recovery and specialist collection separately scoped.

### Acquisition

- owner/CFO/finance manager outreach only after founder approval;
- accountant/bookkeeper/referral channel;
- ageing-ledger diagnostic using client-authorised data;
- sell the 30-day operating outcome, not generic AI or reminder software.

### Delivery

```text
accounting/ERP ledger
→ ageing/priority policy
→ deterministic reminder cadence
→ bounded AI classification/prioritisation
→ selected bilingual human escalation
→ promise/dispute/payment tracking
→ system-of-record update
→ cash/DSO/contribution report
```

## Counter-evidence / stop conditions

Low-cost UAE software is strong, so software-only reminders are not the product. Hold/recycle if phone chasing, legal escalation or bespoke disputes dominate human time, or if managed-service WTP collapses toward commodity software pricing.

## One Next Proof

Complete one 30-day cycle and measure attributable cash/ageing change, human escalation minutes, tool/channel cost, disputes, complaints, gross contribution and renewal intent.
