## Business definition

> We sell a managed company/workforce compliance desk to UAE SMEs for onboarding plus a monthly retainer because visas, work permits, Emirates IDs, licences and related government deadlines recur continuously but many SMEs do not need a full-time in-house PRO. Revenue arrives as setup + recurring managed operations, while government and authorised-provider fees remain separate.

The product is **not** an expiry-reminder app and does not replace MoHRE, ICP, free-zone or other authority systems.

## First offer — AI PRO & Workforce Compliance Desk

Core recurring scope:

- company/workforce compliance register;
- document OCR and completeness checks;
- visa/work-permit/Emirates-ID/trade-licence/establishment-card calendar;
- transaction packs/checklists;
- employee/client reminders and approvals;
- authorised PRO/provider coordination;
- status/exception management;
- evidence archive and monthly upcoming-actions report.

## Forward-test price

- **AED1,500 onboarding/setup**;
- **AED1,500/month** bounded core retainer for up to roughly 20 staff / defined normal transaction volume;
- **AED2,500–4,000/month** larger/denser environments;
- government, medical, insurance, typing and authorised-provider fees separate/pass-through.

These are estimates, not accepted-customer prices.

2. target founder/COO/office manager/HR lead;

4. use warm business relationships plus company-formation/accounting/HR partners;
5. sell accountable recurring operations, not AI novelty.

## Delivery architecture

```text
client-owned company / employee record
→ OCR + deterministic deadlines / checklists
→ bounded AI completeness / exception analysis
→ authorised human/PRO/provider review
→ client approval for consequential submission/payment
→ official authority / free-zone / licensed provider
→ status + evidence back to client record
```

Do not impersonate a client, bypass UAE PASS/authority controls or make unqualified legal/immigration guarantees.

## Hard boundaries / counter-evidence

- government and low-cost software automation will keep removing manual steps;
- basic expiry reminders are commodity features;
- UAE PASS/client credentials and authorised signatory/provider boundaries must be respected;
- sensitive identity/workforce data requires governed handling;
- unusual immigration/legal/tax issues must route to competent authorised providers;
- scope creep into company formation, accounting, payroll and legal work must be separately defined/priced.
