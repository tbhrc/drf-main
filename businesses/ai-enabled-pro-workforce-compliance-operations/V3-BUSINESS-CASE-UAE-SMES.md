## Market-ready offer

**AI PRO & Workforce Compliance Desk**

Promise: maintain one current company/workforce compliance record, prepare upcoming transactions early, coordinate approved submissions/providers and give management evidence/status without needing a full-time PRO.

### Forward-test commercial model

- **AED1,500 onboarding/setup**;
- **AED1,500/month** core bounded retainer up to roughly 20 staff / defined normal transaction volume;
- **AED2,500–4,000/month** higher-volume/complex environments;
- government and authorised-provider fees separate/pass-through.

### Acquisition

- founder/COO/office-manager/HR decision makers;
- 90-day compliance-event diagnostic;
- warm SME relationships plus company-formation/accountant/HR partner channels;
- sell accountable managed operations rather than AI or reminder software.

### Delivery

```text
client-owned company/employee record
→ OCR + deterministic deadlines/checklists
→ bounded AI completeness/exception analysis
→ authorised human/PRO/provider review
→ client approval
→ authority/free-zone/provider execution
→ status/evidence archive
→ monthly upcoming-actions report
```

## Counter-evidence / stop conditions

Commodity compliance trackers cost tens to low hundreds of dirhams per month and UAE authorities are rapidly automating services. The business fails if it becomes a manual portal/typing service without enough managed-accountability value to sustain a recurring premium.
