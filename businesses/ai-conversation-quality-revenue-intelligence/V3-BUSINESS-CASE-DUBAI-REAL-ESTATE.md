## Founder decision

**Advance a bounded paid transferability test.** The broad call/conversation QA category is already commercially proven. DRF should not test whether AI can transcribe or score calls. Test only whether a Dubai brokerage will pay for a calibrated managed layer, whether lawful call data is accessible, whether managers act on the outputs, and whether delivery remains reusable and profitable.

## Selected niche

**Dubai real-estate brokerages / developer sales teams with 20–100 agents, meaningful lead/commission economics and an existing lawful call-recording stack — 88/100 / 86% confidence.**

DLD reported AED13.59bn of brokerage commissions in 2025; Q1 2026 Dubai real-estate transactions reached AED252bn. UAE brokerage software already sells call recording/analytics, and local AI sales-monitoring evidence demonstrates the operating pattern. The exact DRF managed-service price and causal revenue lift remain unproven.

## Offer

### Sales Conversation QA & Revenue Intelligence Layer

Deliver:

- existing-call-source integration;
- transcript / metadata normalisation;
- calibrated role-specific scorecards;
- 100% first-pass AI scoring where safe;
- objection/buying-signal/next-step/process findings;
- team/agent trends and evidence clips;
- coaching/action digest;
- monthly revenue/process insight report;
- bounded human calibration/dispute review.

Do not replace the client's CRM/telephony unless there is a separately proven reason.

## Price / revenue model

Forward test:

- **AED5,000–10,000 setup/calibration**;
- **AED3,000–7,500/month managed recurring service**;
- material high-volume storage/transcription/telephony passed through or usage-priced.

No customer has accepted this price yet.

## GTM

1. Build a qualified Dubai brokerage/developer-sales list from lawful business data.
2. Filter for 20–100 agents and current business telephony/call-recording capability.
3. Lead with a 100-call leakage/quality diagnostic, not an AI demo.
4. Sell one team/office at the price floor.
5. Expand only if managers use the findings and the same scorecard/operating pattern transfers.

Outbound execution is outside this research-only run and remains founder-controlled.

## Delivery architecture

`existing lawful call source → ingestion → transcript/redaction → deterministic client rubric → bounded AI score/evidence extraction → calibration/dispute queue → manager coaching/actions → CRM/revenue outcome join → recurring trend/contribution report`

## Material risks / counter-evidence

- native platform substitution;
- covert/invalid call recording or retention;
- UAE telemarketing/privacy obligations;
- AI scoring disagreements and manager mistrust;
- bespoke rubric/integration work reducing margin;
- analytics without manager action creating low renewal value.

## Exactly one Next Proof

**One paid Dubai brokerage/developer-sales team at ≥AED5,000 setup + ≥AED3,000/month, using ≥500 lawfully recorded calls or 30 days of representative volume.**

**Pass:** ≥80% safe first-pass automation after calibration, bounded human review, ≥3 manager-validated actionable findings, positive expected contribution, and renewal or second-client reuse intent.  
**Hold/recycle:** native tooling already solves it, lawful usable data is unavailable, or integration/calibration remains bespoke enough to destroy recurring margin.
