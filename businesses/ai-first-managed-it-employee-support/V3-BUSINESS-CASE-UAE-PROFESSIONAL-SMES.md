## Founder decision

Advance a bounded **AI-First M365 IT Department** test. Do not build an MSP platform. Use current tools plus qualified partner escalation and prove support economics before adding infrastructure.

## Offer

- employee helpdesk;
- M365 user/licence/access administration;
- joiner/leaver workflows;
- approved device-health/patching automation;
- bounded AI/deterministic remediation;
- monthly health report;
- human/partner escalation for privileged, on-site, security and specialist exceptions.

**Forward price:** AED2,500 onboarding + AED149/user/month, 10-user minimum. Licences, on-site and major/security projects separate.

## Acquisition

Warm Talent Bridge/iMPLEMENTAi relationships first; then owner/COO/office-manager outbound and local-search demand. Sell predictable IT and avoiding a full internal IT hire, not AI novelty.

## Delivery

`request/alert → identity/policy check → deterministic approved workflow → bounded AI → privileged approval → human/partner exception → audit/ticket → monthly report`

## Return

No DRF revenue actual. Desk illustration: 5 × 20-user clients at AED149/user = AED14,900 MRR plus onboarding before human/tooling/on-site cost.

## Main risks

Dense UAE MSP competition; privileged-access liability; hardware/on-site/higher-risk cyber work; possible labour creep; client data/confidentiality; tool-vendor dependence.

## Next Proof

Two unrelated UAE professional-services SMEs pay/commit at the forward commercial floor and complete a 30-day service sample. Pass only if human escalation is bounded, no material security/control failure occurs and expected contribution is positive.
