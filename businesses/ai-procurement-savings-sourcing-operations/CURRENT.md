## Business definition

> We sell recurring procurement savings and sourcing operations to UAE businesses for setup + monthly managed-service/software revenue and, only when attribution is clean, an optional share of verified realised savings. The product runs supplier/RFQ preparation, quote normalisation, negotiation support, approval routing, PO draft/handoff and recurring vendor/renewal operations while human approval remains mandatory for financial commitments.

This is **buyer-side procurement**. It is distinct from DRF's distributor order-desk niche, which automates inbound customer sales orders into the distributor's ERP.

## Risks / boundaries

- Free/low-cost RFQ tools reject a generic workflow-only offer.
- Do not assume METIS's supplier network or enterprise data transfers to DRF.
- Savings fees require explicit baseline/attribution.
- Human approval remains mandatory before material supplier/price/credit/PO commitments.
- Legal contract review, strategic suppliers and urgent site needs stay human.
- Keep ERP/accounting system of record; do not rebuild procurement infrastructure prematurely.
