## Business definition

> We sell continuously maintained, human-reviewed bookkeeping / close / compliance-readiness operations to UAE SMEs for a monthly subscription because recurring finance administration, VAT/Corporate Tax readiness and clean monthly books are necessary but do not justify an internal finance team for many SMEs. Revenue arrives as onboarding/cleanup + recurring monthly managed service, with specialist tax/audit costs separated.

This is not a claim of autonomous accounting and not a tax-agent business by default.

## Why it is a distinct DRF parent

The payer, outcome, recurring compliance/close cadence and human-accountant boundary are specific enough to form an independent commercial model. It is not merely another generic Vertical AI Operating System.

The current external market already proves:

- recurring UAE bookkeeping willingness to pay from sub-AED500 entry tiers to several-thousand-AED managed packages;
- UAE-local AI/accounting automation products and services including Pinto, CrossVal, MIZAN, Rasid and Accountech4u;
- a close analogue in Pinto: managed WhatsApp bookkeeping with AI processing and licensed-accountant review;
- strong regulatory reasons to keep material tax representation and judgement inside qualified human / registered-agent boundaries.

## First offer

### AI-Assisted Finance Operations Desk

Core recurring scope:

- invoice / receipt / document intake via approved channels;
- transaction classification and reconciliation;
- monthly-close checklist and exception queue;
- P&L / balance sheet plus basic receivables/payables/cash visibility;
- VAT / Corporate Tax readiness and missing-evidence flags;
- evidence-linked anomaly review;
- qualified-accountant review of material exceptions/final output;
- registered-tax-agent / audit partner handoff when regulated representation or specialist sign-off is required.

Do not market DRF/iMPLEMENTAi as an FTA Tax Agent unless the required registration actually exists.

## Price hypothesis

Current UAE public pricing supports a wide market band. DRF forward-test hypothesis:

- **AED500–2,000 one-time cleanup/onboarding** depending on book quality and transaction volume;
- **AED1,250–2,500/month** for a bounded 30–150 transaction managed finance-operations package;
- regulated tax-agent, audit and specialist-advisory costs separate and transparent.

These are estimates, not accepted-customer prices.

## GTM

Prioritise low-cost routes:

1. qualified existing business relationships where the ICP genuinely fits;
2. partnerships with registered tax agents/accountants/company-formation ecosystems;
3. owner/founder LinkedIn and local search around close readiness, VAT/CT hygiene and e-Invoicing preparation;
4. a bounded finance-operations readiness / reconciliation diagnostic rather than generic AI messaging.

## Delivery architecture

```text
client documents / bank / approved accounting system
→ deterministic extraction / reconciliation rules first
→ bounded AI classification / exception analysis
→ human accountant review for material judgement
→ management reporting / close evidence
→ registered tax-agent or auditor partner when regulated representation/sign-off is required
```

Existing accounting software remains the system of record. Do not rebuild a ledger.

## Hard boundaries / counter-evidence

- FTA tax-agent practice/representation is regulated; do not cross that boundary without proper registration/partner structure.
- UAE bookkeeping is price-competitive; “AI bookkeeping” alone is not differentiated.
- Financial records are sensitive; live pilots require governed access/storage/confidentiality.
- Poor historical books can destroy margin; cleanup is separately scoped/paid.
- Native accounting platforms keep improving; value must sit in the managed outcome, exception resolution, evidence and responsiveness.
