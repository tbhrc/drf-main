## Offer

A specialist Talent Bridge recruitment business focused on Agentic AI / Forward Deployed AI engineering roles, differentiated by a live AI-native proof-of-work assessment, auditable candidate evidence packs, Talent Bridge verification and a curated accepted-only candidate directory.

## Outcome sold

Employers get faster access to a small number of engineers whose practical production Agentic AI capability has already been observed, scored and evidenced.

## Commercial model

Primary revenue is direct-hire placement/search fee revenue, with optional later contract margin, retained access, talent-intelligence or standalone assessment revenue only after employer trust and supply quality are proven.

## Current decision

Advance to TEST. Do not build the full marketplace first. Prove the assessment standard, candidate quality and employer trust through a 10-candidate v0 cohort and targeted employer validation.

## Next Proof

Run a 10-candidate v0 Agentic AI Engineer live proof-of-work assessment cohort. Pass only if the cohort produces at least 3 credible verified candidates, at least 2 qualified employer interview/conversation requests, and at least 1 employer willing to proceed on paid Talent Bridge commercial terms.
