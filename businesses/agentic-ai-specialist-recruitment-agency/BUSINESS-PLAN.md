# Business Plan — Agentic AI Specialist Recruitment Agency

**Date:** 10 September 2026  
**Governing issue:** [#184](https://github.com/tbhrc/drf-main/issues/184)  
**Current decision:** TEST  
**Canonical current state:** [`CURRENT.md`](./CURRENT.md)

## 1. Executive summary

Talent Bridge should build a specialist recruitment business for Agentic AI / Forward Deployed AI engineers, starting in Dubai/UAE and expanding globally once the assessment standard and employer trust are proven.

The business does not begin as a job board or generic candidate database. It begins as a high-trust specialist search/recruitment service where every publicly promoted candidate has passed a live AI-native proof-of-work assessment and has an auditable evidence pack.

## 2. Strategic thesis

Talent Bridge can own a narrow, premium category where three existing strengths compound:

```text
Recruitment execution
+ assessment/evaluation expertise
+ practical Agentic AI engineering credibility
= verified Agentic AI talent supply business
```

This is the strongest bridge between Talent Bridge and iMPLEMENTAi because the recruitment offer itself is improved by AI engineering expertise.

## 3. Customer and ICP

### Beachhead ICP

UAE/GCC AI consultancies, systems integrators and enterprise AI delivery teams hiring production Agentic AI / Forward Deployed AI Engineers against live client/project demand.

### Buyer roles

- Founder / CEO / Managing Partner;
- CTO / Head of Engineering;
- Head of AI / Digital Transformation;
- Delivery Director / Practice Lead;
- Talent Acquisition / HR only after technical buyer alignment.

## 4. Customer pain

Employers struggle to identify real production AI engineers because CVs, polished interviews and generic coding tests do not reveal whether a candidate can:

- frame ambiguous business problems;
- design reliable agent workflows;
- use AI tools intelligently;
- evaluate model outputs;
- handle context, RAG, tools, memory and state;
- ship maintainable production work;
- reason about cost, latency, privacy and failure;
- communicate trade-offs to stakeholders.

## 5. Product / service

### Core v1 service

**Verified Agentic AI Engineer Shortlist**

- role brief and scorecard;
- targeted sourcing;
- candidate proof reconstruction from CV, LinkedIn, GitHub and shipped work;
- live AI-native assessment;
- fixed competency rubric;
- human technical QA;
- Talent Bridge Verified decision;
- evidence-backed shortlist;
- interview and placement process management.

### Directory / marketplace

The public profile directory is a later demand-generation surface, not the starting product. It should list only accepted candidates with explicit consent and no private phone/email exposure.

## 6. Assessment IP

The assessment is the defensibility engine. It must test real work, not paper claims.

Recommended structure:

```text
role-specific scenario
→ live AI-assisted execution
→ output/repository/log/transcript capture
→ adaptive change/failure prompt
→ explanation and trade-off review
→ rubric scoring
→ human technical QA
→ evidence pack
```

## 7. Commercial model

### Primary

- Direct-hire/search fee: **18% of first-year fixed cash compensation**.
- Suggested minimum successful-placement fee: **AED40,000**.

### Later options

- contract margin;
- retained/exclusive search;
- employer talent-access subscription;
- standalone evidence-pack assessment;
- certification renewal / revalidation;
- talent intelligence reports.

Do not sell candidate-paid access as the starting model.

## 9. Go-to-market

### First 10 employer route

1. Build a 50-account UAE/GCC target list.
2. Select 10 highest-fit employers with current AI delivery hiring pressure.
3. Run v0 assessment cohort first.
4. Present anonymised/permissioned evidence packs rather than generic CVs.
5. Ask for one live role, paid placement terms or paid assessment/shortlist pilot.

### Messaging

> We do not send AI CVs. We send engineers whose Agentic AI execution has already been observed, scored and evidenced.

## 10. Operating model

```text
Talent Bridge / David category lead
→ sourcing + candidate ops
→ ALA/live AI assessment interviewer
→ fixed rubric + evidence extraction
→ human technical QA
→ candidate verification decision
→ employer shortlist + placement process
→ outcome feedback loop
```

The assessment evidence corpus should become a reusable asset: rubric refinements, benchmark profiles, false-positive lessons, employer feedback, placement outcomes and renewal/retention signals.

## 11. Legal, privacy and fairness controls

Minimum v0 controls:

- explicit candidate consent/privacy notice;
- public profile consent separate from private assessment evidence;
- no phone/email public by default;
- no emotion/accent/personality inference;
- fixed competency rubric;
- human review before commercial verification/rejection;
- candidate correction/retest path;
- data retention and security boundary;
- avoid “accredited certification” language unless legally supported.

## 12. Milestones

| Phase | Goal | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| v0 | 10-candidate cohort | ≥3 pass; ≥2 employer conversations; ≥1 paid employer commitment |
| v1 | first paid placement/search | one paid role or placement with evidence pack used in decision |
| v2 | accepted-only profile page | public profiles only after employer pull and candidate consent |
| v3 | repeatability | 3 paid mandates or placements in same role family |
| v4 | scaled supply | recurring verified-candidate inflow and employer repeat demand |

## 13. Main risks

- specialist incumbents already exist;
- global vetted marketplaces already exist;
- assessment trust must be earned;
- marketplace liquidity is hard;
- candidate data is sensitive;
- human review can become the bottleneck;
- if pass rate is too low, supply is too thin;
- if pass rate is too high, the brand loses exclusivity.

## 14. Exact next action

Run the **10-candidate v0 Agentic AI Engineer assessment cohort** and test the resulting evidence packs with qualified UAE/GCC employers before any marketplace build.
