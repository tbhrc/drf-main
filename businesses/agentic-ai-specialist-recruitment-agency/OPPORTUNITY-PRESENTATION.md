## 1. Decision

**Advance to TEST.**

Build the v0 proof-of-work assessment and use it to create a verified candidate shortlist before building a public marketplace.

---

## 2. The opportunity

Dubai and the UAE are accelerating Agentic AI adoption. Employers need engineers who can actually build, integrate and operate production AI agents — not just talk about AI or present polished AI-generated CVs.

Talent Bridge can own the premium recruitment lane by supplying already-tested Agentic AI engineers.

---

## 3. The problem

AI hiring is noisy:

- CVs are inflated;
- generic recruiters cannot validate technical depth;
- ordinary interviews miss AI-native execution;
- coding tests do not show agent design, evaluation, security or recovery judgement;
- false positives are expensive.

---

## 4. The wedge

**Live AI-native proof-of-work assessment.**

Candidates are allowed to use AI. The assessment observes whether they can use it properly:

- frame the problem;
- design the agent/workflow;
- implement and test;
- evaluate outputs;
- handle failure/change;
- explain trade-offs.

---

## 5. The product

**Verified Agentic AI Engineer Shortlist**

Employers receive a small shortlist of candidates who have passed Talent Bridge's evidence standard, with private proof packs and role-specific scoring.

---

## 6. Why Talent Bridge can win

The blend is unusually strong:

```text
Recruitment
+ Assessments
+ Agentic AI engineering credibility
= Verified Agentic AI Talent business
```

The assessment is not a generic test; it is built from practical Agentic AI work.

## 14. Kill / recycle triggers

Recycle or hold if:

- fewer than 3 credible candidates pass v0;
- employers do not trust the evidence;
- human review exceeds 2 hours per candidate;
- no employer interest after 20 qualified conversations;
- data/privacy/fairness risk cannot be controlled.
