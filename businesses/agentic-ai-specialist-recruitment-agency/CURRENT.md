## Business definition

> We sell rapid access to independently proof-of-work-verified Agentic AI engineers to UAE/GCC employers for placement/search fees and optional recurring talent-access or contract margins because CVs, generic recruiters and conventional interviews do not reliably prove production Agentic AI engineering capability.

## First offer

### Verified Agentic AI Engineer Shortlist

- role calibration for Agentic AI / Forward Deployed AI engineering;
- candidate sourcing and reconstruction from CV, LinkedIn, GitHub and proof-of-work artefacts;
- live AI-native proof-of-work assessment;
- fixed competency rubric and evidence pack;
- bounded human technical review;
- accepted-only Talent Bridge verification;
- 3–5 role-matched verified candidates where supply allows;
- Talent Bridge-managed introduction, interview process and placement.

## Forward price test

- **Direct hire / search:** 18% of first-year fixed cash compensation, suggested minimum AED40,000 per successful placement.
- **Example desk economics:** AED360,000 first-year salary × 18% = AED64,800 placement revenue.
- **Assessment-only option:** AED1,500 per candidate only when strategically useful; not the primary v0 revenue path.
- **Talent access subscription:** defer until there is enough accepted supply and employer pull to justify recurring access.

These are test terms, not accepted-customer actuals.

## GTM

1. Build a 50-account UAE/GCC target list of AI consultancies, systems integrators, enterprise AI/digital-transformation teams and funded AI-native companies.
2. Lead with the role evidence problem, not a generic recruitment pitch.
3. Offer a short evidence-backed market map and a small verified shortlist for one urgent role.
4. Use the first v0 cohort to create anonymised/permissioned candidate proof examples.
5. Convert via paid placement/search terms before building marketplace infrastructure.

## Delivery architecture

```text
employer role brief
→ role rubric + scenario design
→ candidate sourcing / proof reconstruction
→ live AI-native work simulation
→ evidence capture: repo/output/logs/transcript/rubric
→ fixed scorecard + bounded human technical review
→ Talent Bridge verification decision
→ permissioned profile / private evidence pack
→ employer shortlist + interview process
→ placement / feedback / outcome tracking
```

AI can assist sourcing, interview adaptation, evidence extraction, report drafting and score explanation. Human review remains mandatory before commercial certification, rejection or employer recommendation.

## Risks / boundaries

- Discovered and other specialist recruiters already occupy Dubai AI recruitment mindshare.
- Braintrust already offers AI skills interviews, certification and vetted profiles globally.
- Public directory without verified supply and employer pull becomes a weak job board.
- AI-assisted assessment can be gamed if output is scored without observing process, changes and recovery.
- Candidate/employer data is sensitive; security and privacy controls are mandatory.
- Employment AI creates fairness, contestability and human-review obligations.
- Avoid claiming accredited certification unless a real accreditation basis exists; use Talent Bridge Verified / Assessed language first.

## Next Proof

**Run a 10-candidate v0 Agentic AI Engineer live proof-of-work assessment cohort before building the marketplace.**

Capture candidate source, role fit, assessment time, AI/tool use, output quality, score, pass/reject reason, human-review minutes, candidate experience and employer response.

**Pass:** at least 3 candidates clear the standard, at least 2 qualified employers request interviews/conversations from the evidence packs, and at least 1 employer agrees to paid Talent Bridge commercial terms.  
**Hold/Recycle:** pass rate is too low, employer trust is weak, review time exceeds 2 hours per candidate, or the evidence pack does not change employer behaviour.  
**Stop:** no employer interest after 20 qualified employer conversations/reviews, or the assessment cannot produce consistent evidence without excessive bespoke review.
