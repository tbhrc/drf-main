## Founder decision

Advance one bounded v0 proof. Do not build the marketplace, public directory or full assessment platform first. Prove that the live assessment produces credible candidate evidence and that employers will act on it.

## Business / money model

> We sell rapid access to independently proof-of-work-verified Agentic AI engineers to UAE/GCC employers for placement/search fees and optional recurring talent-access or contract margins because CVs, generic recruiters and conventional interviews do not reliably prove production Agentic AI engineering capability.

## Layer 1 assessment

The opportunity scores **90/100** structurally because demand/timing are strong, startup cost is low, placement economics are meaningful and the founder has unusual category credibility. It is not scored higher because recruitment remains partly human-led, incumbent recruiters already exist and recurring revenue quality is weaker than native SaaS until talent access or contract-margin models are proven.

## External Market Proof

**EMP4 / 94%.** The exact Talent Bridge model is not yet proven, but the core components are externally established:

- Dubai/UAE Agentic AI adoption and hiring demand;
- Dubai specialist AI/data recruitment incumbents;
- elite screened talent networks;
- AI skills interview / certification / verified talent-profile patterns;
- practical work simulations and live technical screens;
- technical-assessment rubrics that evaluate Human + AI engineering judgement.

## Counter-evidence

- Discovered already has credible Dubai AI/data recruitment positioning.
- Braintrust already combines AI skills interviews, certification and vetted profiles globally.
- Employer trust must be earned; a self-issued badge has no value without predictive evidence.
- Public talent directory liquidity is risky before transactions exist.
- Employment AI creates privacy, fairness, contestability and human-review obligations.

## Selected niche

**UAE/GCC AI consultancies, systems integrators and enterprise AI delivery teams hiring production Agentic AI / Forward Deployed AI Engineers against live project demand.**

Why this first:

- urgent project/revenue pressure;
- repeated specialist hiring possible;
- technical false positives are expensive;
- decision-makers are identifiable;
- the role requires the observable practical competencies the assessment can test.

Runner-up: Dubai AI-native startups/scaleups hiring senior/founding production AI engineers after funding or product expansion.

## Offer

**Verified Agentic AI Engineer Shortlist**

- role calibration;
- targeted sourcing;
- live AI-native proof-of-work assessment;
- auditable candidate evidence pack;
- Talent Bridge verification decision;
- 3–5 role-matched candidates where supply allows;
- employer shortlist/interview process management;
- placement/search delivery.

## GTM

1. Build a 50-account UAE/GCC employer list across AI consultancies, systems integrators, enterprise AI teams and funded AI-native businesses.
2. Run the v0 candidate cohort first to create real evidence packs.
3. Approach employers with anonymised/permissioned proof examples and a narrow role proposition.
4. Convert to paid search/placement terms before public marketplace build.
5. Use employer feedback to calibrate the rubric and pass bar.

## Delivery architecture

`role brief → role rubric → candidate sourcing/proof reconstruction → live AI-native work simulation → evidence capture → scorecard → bounded human technical review → Talent Bridge verification → permissioned profile/evidence pack → employer shortlist → placement/outcome feedback`

Human review remains mandatory before commercial verification, rejection or employer recommendation.

## Next Proof

Run a **10-candidate v0 Agentic AI Engineer live proof-of-work assessment cohort**.

**Pass:** at least 3 candidates clear the standard, at least 2 qualified employers request interviews/conversations from the evidence packs, and at least 1 employer agrees to paid Talent Bridge commercial terms.

**Hold/Recycle:** pass quality is poor, employer trust is weak, human review exceeds 2 hours per candidate, or evidence packs do not change employer behaviour.

**Stop:** no employer interest after 20 qualified employer conversations/reviews, or assessment cannot produce consistent signal without excessive bespoke review.
