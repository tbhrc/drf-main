## External proof and counter-evidence

HighLevel's current case studies show multiple recurring SaaS operators using SaaS Mode and snapshots, including Business Done Better (~200 customers, US$199/month SaaS), AR Funnel (350+ recurring clients, US$1,000 upfront + US$497/month and >US$200k MRR by year three), AdsExpress (115+ recurring clients on US$107–397 tiers) and The Lead King (~120 sub-accounts producing about US$12k MRR after a distribution event).

HighLevel's current official product surfaces also support automated SaaS subscriptions/account creation, snapshots, usage rebilling/markup and Snapshot Marketplace one-time/monthly/yearly sales.

Counter-evidence is material: AR Funnel documented early churn up to 24% before rebuilding onboarding/support; generic snapshots are cheap; platform dependency is real; and a raw white-label CRM has weak differentiation. The DRF package therefore sells a specific HVAC operating outcome and instruments support/usage.
