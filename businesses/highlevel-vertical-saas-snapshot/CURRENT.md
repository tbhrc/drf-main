This is strong multi-operator market proof. It is not EMP4 because most detailed success evidence is platform/vendor-published and the exact UAE HVAC transferability is not yet proven.

## Counter-evidence

The model is not low-touch by default:

- AR Funnel documents high early churn and a later support/onboarding rebuild involving live chat and office hours;
- generic snapshot libraries are inexpensive and increasingly commoditised;
- a platform outage, agency suspension or dependency can create customer lock-in/data-continuity risk;
- SaaS resale without a vertical result becomes undifferentiated CRM access;
- communication, AI and premium-feature usage creates variable cost and support exceptions.

The DRF offer therefore has to sell a **working vertical operating outcome** and deliberately control onboarding/support, not merely white-label the login page.
