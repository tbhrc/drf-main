## Atomic niche

`Verified Agentic AI Engineer shortlist × AI consultancies/systems integrators/enterprise AI delivery teams × UAE/GCC × live project hiring pressure × employer cannot reliably assess production Agentic AI capability`

## Buyer

- founder / managing partner of AI consultancy;
- CTO / head of engineering;
- head of AI / digital transformation;
- delivery director / practice lead;
- enterprise AI team hiring manager;
- talent acquisition only after technical buyer alignment.

## Core pain

These employers need engineers who can deliver production Agentic AI work, but the candidate market is noisy. CVs and ordinary interviews do not reliably prove the practical ability to design, ship, evaluate and govern agentic workflows.

## Recommended offer for this niche

**Verified Agentic AI Engineer Shortlist**

Deliver 3–5 role-matched candidates where supply allows, each with:

- Talent Bridge Verified status;
- private evidence pack;
- proof-of-work scorecard;
- prior project/GitHub/proof review where available;
- interview-readiness notes;
- risk flags / validation notes.

## Price test

- 18% of first-year fixed cash compensation;
- suggested AED40,000 minimum successful-placement fee;
- optional paid shortlist/assessment service only if employer wants evidence before a success-fee placement process.

## Acquisition plan

1. Build 50-account UAE/GCC target list.
2. Prioritise visible Agentic AI / GenAI / Forward Deployed AI hiring signals.
3. Use v0 cohort evidence examples rather than generic pitch copy.
4. Ask for one active role or paid trial mandate.
5. Track employer trust in the evidence pack.

## Delivery architecture

`role brief → role-specific rubric → candidate sourcing/proof reconstruction → live AI-native proof-of-work → evidence pack → human QA → accepted-only shortlist → employer interview/placement process`

## Failure modes

- buyer sees no difference from a specialist AI recruiter;
- employer does not trust Talent Bridge technical verification;
- accepted supply is too thin;
- candidate assessment takes too long;
- pass/fail consistency is weak;
- candidate privacy/public-profile controls are inadequate.

## Next evidence

The next evidence is not more desk research. It is a **10-candidate v0 cohort** plus employer review of the resulting evidence packs.

**Pass:** at least 3 verified candidates, at least 2 qualified employer interview/conversation requests and at least 1 paid employer commitment.  
**Hold/Recycle:** weak pass quality, weak employer trust or excessive review burden.  
**Stop:** no employer interest after 20 qualified conversations/reviews.
