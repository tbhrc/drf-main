## Why this is the first beachhead

- M365-centric support and identity workflows are highly standardisable.
- Firms are large enough to pay recurring IT support but small enough to lack specialist internal IT.
- UAE public managed-IT pricing proves existing budget.
- Decision makers are owner/COO/office manager level and directly targetable.
- Remote-first fulfilment is practical for a high share of routine requests.
- Human partner escalation can cover exceptional/on-site work without staffing a full internal helpdesk before demand exists.

## Current substitutes

Traditional UAE IT AMC/MSPs, break-fix IT, Microsoft partners, internal office/admin staff and emerging AI helpdesk tooling.

The product must therefore outperform on bounded response, automation, auditability and predictable ownership — not on an AI label.

## Offer / price / GTM

**Offer:** AI-First M365 IT Department.  
**Forward test:** AED2,500 onboarding + AED149/user/month; 10-user minimum; licences/on-site/security projects separate.  
**GTM:** warm business relationships first, then owner/COO/office-manager outbound and local-search demand.

## Safety / delivery rule

Routine requests may be deterministic/AI-first. Privileged changes require scoped permissions and approval; on-site, hardware, network, cybersecurity incidents and high-risk exceptions go to qualified human/partner delivery.

## Next Proof

Two unrelated UAE firms pay/commit at the test floor and complete a 30-day sample showing bounded human escalation, safe administration and positive expected contribution.
