## Current offer

**AI PRO & Workforce Compliance Desk** — AED1,500 onboarding + AED1,500/month bounded core retainer; larger environments AED2,500–4,000/month; government/provider fees separate.

## Comparable proof

- Riz & Mona publishes AED1,500–6,000/month PRO retainers.
- Emirates Compliance sells AI/OCR workforce-compliance software at AED8–12/employee/month.
- Proziyo digitises UAE PRO tasks for service firms and in-house teams.
- DocuBay demonstrates AI preparation + customer approval + licensed-provider routing.
- MoHRE/ICP first-party evidence shows authority services are rapidly automating, which narrows the value proposition toward preparation/accountability/exceptions.

## Counter-evidence

Basic reminders/trackers are commodity-priced and government systems keep removing manual work. The niche only works if managed coordination and exception ownership remain valuable while human/provider minutes stay bounded.
