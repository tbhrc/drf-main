## Current evidence

- Dubai Land Department / Media Office: brokerage commissions **AED13.59bn in 2025**, up 31%; broker-executed transactions **96,440**, up 54%.
- DLD: Q1 2026 total real-estate transactions **AED252bn**, up 31% year-on-year in value.
- SkyOS markets UAE brokerage call recording, agent monitoring and analytics, showing that the workflow/data surface already exists locally.
- Smart AMC publicly describes a 14-agent Dubai real-estate AI sales-monitoring deployment; its performance claims are first-party only and are not used as a base rate.
- Observe.AI, Call Optix, TotalView and Enthu establish the broader recurring conversation-QA market.

## Buyer / trigger / outcome

**Payer:** owner, sales director, COO or head of sales operations.  
**Trigger:** growing team, expensive leads, inconsistent conversion, weak coaching visibility, new projects/agents, or compliance/process concerns.  
**Outcome:** comprehensively score existing calls and turn evidence into repeatable coaching/process/revenue actions without replacing the existing CRM or telephony.

## Incumbents / substitutes

- native call/CRM analytics;
- manual manager review;
- dedicated QA staff;
- generic transcription/meeting AI;
- enterprise conversation-intelligence suites;
- AI calling platforms with built-in analytics.

A prospect with already-calibrated native QA/action should be disqualified rather than sold another dashboard.

## Compliance boundary

For UAE telephone marketing, Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 requires, among other controls, DNCR/time restrictions, call-recording notice and records. The first DRF test must use a client's existing lawful recording basis and approved retention/access. Financial-sector telemarketing has additional CBUAE requirements and is excluded from the first niche.

## Offer / price / GTM

**Offer:** Sales Conversation QA & Revenue Intelligence Layer.  
**Forward price:** AED5,000–10,000 setup + AED3,000–7,500/month; usage/pass-through for material high-volume costs.  
**GTM:** 100-call diagnostic from an existing lawful sample → evidence findings → one-team paid pilot → expand only after manager usage and stable calibration.

## Delivery

`lawful recorded calls → transcript/metadata → fixed client rubric → AI first-pass scoring + evidence → calibration/dispute queue → manager action/coaching → outcome join → recurring trends`

## Decision

**Sniper — TEST.** The niche is strong enough for a bounded paid transferability test; it is not yet local price/retention proof.

## Next Proof

One unrelated Dubai brokerage/developer-sales team paying at least **AED5,000 setup + AED3,000/month** with ≥500 lawful recorded calls or 30 days of representative volume.

Pass only if first-pass automation reaches ≥80% after calibration, review/support stays bounded, managers validate at least three actionable findings, expected contribution is positive and renewal/second-client reuse interest exists.
