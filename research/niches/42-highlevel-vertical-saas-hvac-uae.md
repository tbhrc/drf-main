## Why this is not a duplicate of the WhatsApp + CRM parent

The buyer and some workflows overlap, but the founder decision is independently testable. This parent asks whether a **platform-led, automatically provisioned, reusable SaaS/snapshot package** can be sold repeatedly with recurring software/rebilling margin and bounded onboarding/support. The WhatsApp + CRM parent remains the outcome/foundation layer and can use other implementation rails.

## Market and operating evidence

- Existing DRF HVAC research already scores the underlying customer/problem as one of the portfolio's strongest service-business markets.
- HighLevel currently prices SaaS Mode at US$497/month and explicitly supports automated subscription/account creation and usage rebilling.
- HighLevel's 2026 Snapshot Marketplace supports one-time, monthly or yearly snapshot sales with IP protection.
- HighLevel case studies document recurring SaaS operators using snapshots/SaaS Mode at US$107–497+ monthly and customer counts from 115 to 350+; Business Done Better serves close to 200 customers using a reusable smart snapshot.

## Counter-evidence

- raw snapshots are commoditised and can sell for a few hundred dollars;
- customers can churn if onboarding/support is weak;
- platform dependence and agency-account continuity are meaningful risks;
- the niche already has software and CRM alternatives;
- bespoke client configuration can erase the leverage promised by snapshots.
